## The widget is blank in OBS

Hit **Refresh** on the browser source. If it happens on every start, check that the URL is
`http://localhost:3001` and that nothing else is on port `3001`.

## Queueify stops on startup saying a port is taken

It is already running in another window. Close that one, or change the port.

::tip{to="/reference/ports-and-env"}
Changing the widget or Canvas port.
::

## `!tr` and `!bc` do nothing

Three things to check, in order:

- the **OBS WebSocket server** is on: **OBS → Tools → WebSocket Server Settings**
- the connect info is pasted into the **OBS** step on the dashboard
- the scene and source are picked **from the dropdowns**, not typed, because they have to match
  OBS exactly

## A viewer cannot move the widget

Mods always can. Anyone else has to be in **Admin Panel → Settings → Whitelist**.

## Channel point requests do nothing

- the reward only exists on Affiliate and Partner channels
- the Twitch account you approved has to be the broadcaster, or a mod on the channel
- check that the reward is enabled with `!rewardon`

## Songs are not queueing

Adding to the playback queue needs **Spotify Premium**, since it is a Premium-only part of
Spotify's API. Also check that the queue is open (`!qon`) and that chat requests are on
(`!chaton`).

## No Canvas videos

The `sp_dc` cookie has expired. Paste a fresh one into the **Canvas videos** step. The dashboard
checks it against Spotify immediately.

::tip{to="/getting-started/connect-accounts"}
Where to find the cookie.
::

## Queueify stops with a message about Node

Node 20 is the minimum. [Update it](https://nodejs.org/en/download) and start it again.
