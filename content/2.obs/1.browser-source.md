### Add a browser source

In OBS, add a **Browser** source to your scene.

### Point it at the widget

```
http://localhost:3001
```

### Give it a size

**Width** `680`, **Height** `192`.

::

::note
If the widget does not show on startup, hit **Refresh** on the source in OBS.
::

## How sizing works

queueify renders each theme at the size it was designed at, and resizes the OBS browser source
to match the space you have given it - when the bot starts, when you switch theme, and when you
drag the widget in OBS. It reloads the source as well, so you do not have to hit **Refresh**
after opening OBS.

That means `680` x `192` only matters until queueify first connects to OBS. After that it sets
the numbers itself.

## Themes with different proportions

Switch to a theme with a different shape - a tall corner panel where a wide strip used to be -
and queueify fits it into the space the old one had instead of stretching it to fill it. It is
not squashed or cropped.
