Every step below happens on the dashboard that `npm start` opens at `http://127.0.0.1:3002`.
Nothing here is typed into a terminal or a config file: each step is a panel on that page, it
writes the files queueify needs, and a tick appears beside the ones that are done.

![The dashboard, with Twitch connected](/img/dashboard.png){.rounded-xl}

The same page holds everything else you would otherwise be editing by hand: widget themes, the
admin panel, the command list, the stats page, and what changed in the version you are running.

| Step | What it does | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Twitch** | Approve queueify on Twitch. Nothing to copy or paste | Yes |
| **Spotify** | Paste a Client ID and Secret, then connect | Yes |
| **Channel points** | Creates the reward viewers redeem to request a song | No |
| **OBS** | Pick your scene and widget source from lists that come from OBS | No |
| **Canvas videos** | Paste the `sp_dc` cookie for Spotify's looping clips | No |

The page stays up for as long as the bot runs. Go back to it to reconnect an account, change
your OBS scene or source, or paste a new Spotify cookie. Changes apply on the next command,
without stopping the bot.

::warning
Whichever Twitch account you approve with is the account the bot chats as. It must be your
broadcaster account or a moderator on your channel, or channel point redemptions will not work.
The panel says so too, next to the channel name.
::

## Spotify

Spotify caps a shared app at 5 users, so you create your own. The dashboard links to it.

::steps{level="3"}

### Create an app

In the [Spotify developer dashboard](https://developer.spotify.com/dashboard), create an app.
The name and description do not matter.

### Set the Redirect URI

```
http://127.0.0.1:8000/callback
```

This has to match exactly, including the port.

### Paste the Client ID and Secret

Both are on the app's settings page. Paste them into the **Spotify** step and connect.

::

::collapsible{name="a filled-in example"}
![A correctly filled in Spotify app](/img/spotify-app-example.png)
::

queueify manages the access and refresh tokens from then on.

## Channel points

Optional, and only possible on Affiliate and Partner channels. The **Channel points** step
creates the reward viewers redeem to request a song, and queueify handles redemptions over
Twitch EventSub in real time.

Redemptions are refunded automatically when the request cannot be honoured:

- the user is on cooldown
- the same-song repeat block is active
- the song is longer than the configured maximum
- the user is on the deny list, or the song or artist is blocked
- the queue is closed

Turn the reward on and off from chat with `!rewardon` and `!rewardoff`.

## Canvas videos

Spotify ships a short looping clip for many tracks, and the widget can play it instead of the
album cover. It needs one cookie and nothing installed.

Paste your `sp_dc` cookie into the **Canvas videos** step. queueify checks it against Spotify
immediately, so an expired cookie is rejected on the page instead of failing later.

::collapsible{name="where to find the sp_dc value"}
Open Spotify in a browser, press :kbd{value="Shift"} + :kbd{value="F9"} to open the **Storage**
tab, and copy the `sp_dc` cookie.

![Where the sp_dc cookie lives](/img/sp-dc-cookie.png)
::

::note
Skip this and the widget shows album art. To turn the clips off for one theme, set **Album
art** to "Album cover only" in the theme editor.
::

## Using your own Twitch application

If you set queueify up with your own Twitch application, it keeps working. The dashboard offers
a one-click switch to the built-in one, which needs no client ID or secret and renews logins
itself. Switching means reconnecting Twitch and recreating the channel point reward once.
