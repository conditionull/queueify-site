The dashboard has a visual editor: **Widget themes → Open the theme editor**, or
`http://127.0.0.1:3002/editor.html`.

Drag the album art, title, artist, progress bar, and the two song times around a canvas,
restyle them, and save. That writes a theme folder under `widget/themes/`, and it shows up in `!theme`
immediately. No restart, no build step.

![The theme editor](/img/theme-editor.png)

## Start from a layout

Three starting layouts: **Classic**, **Spiffy** or **Stacked**.

Album art, title, artist, progress bar, time elapsed, and song length are always part of a theme,
because the widget fills them in. Hide the ones a design does not need rather than deleting
them, including the background panel if you want the text over gameplay.

## Text

::field-group
  ::field{name="Fonts" type="50 available"}
  Plus UPPERCASE and italic.
  ::

  ::field{name="Colors" type="fixed or from the art"}
  Solid, gradient, or sampled from the album art of the current track.
  ::

  ::field{name="Outline" type="any color"}
  So text stays readable over gameplay.
  ::

  ::field{name="Glow" type="all sides"}
  Spreads evenly around the letters.
  ::

  ::field{name="Drop shadow" type="angle, blur, color"}
  Offset in any direction. A blur of `0` gives a hard offset copy.
  ::
::

## Icons

Over 2,000 icons from [Lucide](https://lucide.dev). Search by name and drag one onto the widget.
Colour, line weight, rotation, and opacity are adjustable, and the colour can follow the album
art.

## The progress bar

Two kinds:

- **Plain** - a bar that fills as the song plays
- **Waveform** - lines of differing heights that fill as it plays. Set how many and how far
  apart; **Shuffle** generates a different shape. The shape is saved with the theme

::waveform-diagram
::

The two song times sit either side of the bar by default, `0:04` on the left and `3:07` on the
right. They are ordinary parts, so you can move, restyle or hide them. Themes made before they
existed load with them hidden.

## Working in the editor

::snap-diagram
::

- **Snapping** - edges and centers line up as you drag, resizing locks to another part's width
  or height, and moving locks to spacing you have already used. Hold :kbd{value="Ctrl"} or
  :kbd{value="Shift"} while dragging to turn it off
- **Revert to last save** discards everything since the last save. Undo still works, and undoes
  the revert itself
- **`!tr` and `!bc` have buttons here**, so you can place the widget without using chat
- The preview is drawn by the same code that writes the theme, using your currently playing
  song and its Canvas video, so it matches what OBS renders

::note
Themes you make live in `widget/themes/` and are left alone by updates.
::
