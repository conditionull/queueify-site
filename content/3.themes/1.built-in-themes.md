Three themes ship with queueify. Switch from **Widget themes** on the dashboard, or with
`!theme <name>` in chat, whichever is closer to hand.

```
!theme swag
```

## Canvas videos

Spotify ships a short looping clip for many tracks, and a theme can play it instead of the
album cover, wherever the album art sits in that theme: in its own spot, or filling the widget
behind your text. It needs the `sp_dc` cookie pasted into the dashboard.

::tip{to="/getting-started/connect-accounts#canvas-videos"}
Where to find the `sp_dc` cookie.
::

Per theme, **Album art** in the editor decides what happens:

- **Canvas video, album cover as fallback** - the clip when the track has one
- **Album cover only** - never a clip, even when one exists

When it fills the widget behind the text, the canvas can also be blurred and dimmed, so a busy
clip reads as a background.
That affects the panel's own artwork only; OBS keeps your gameplay behind the widget untouched.
