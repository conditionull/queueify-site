queueify comes with 18 premade themes. Each one is a starting point: pick one, change anything
you like, and save it as a theme of your own. The premades themselves never change.

![The premade theme picker](/img/premade-picker.png){width="1600" height="900" .rounded-xl}

## Opening the picker

In the theme editor, click **Premade themes** in the top bar, or **Premade themes…** under the
canvas. If the theme you have open has unsaved changes, the editor asks before it opens.

## Picking one

- The list is grouped by shape: **Banner**, **Panel**, and **Portrait**. The shape decides
  whether a theme fits the space you have for it in your scene
- The theme you pick plays the song you're playing, at the size it will be in OBS. One too big
  for the preview says how far it's scaled down
- Switch the backdrop between **Dark**, **Light**, and **Clear** to see how it sits on different
  scenes. **Clear** is a checkerboard, so you can see which parts have nothing behind them
- Search by name, or by what a theme has in it, such as "pixel" or "up next"

**Use this theme** opens it as a new theme. Nothing you already have changes. Give it a name
and save it.

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| :kbd{value="↑"} :kbd{value="↓"} | Move through the list, even while typing in the search box |
| :kbd{value="Home"} :kbd{value="End"} | Jump to the first or last theme |
| :kbd{value="Enter"} | Use the theme you picked |
| :kbd{value="Esc"} | Clear the search, then close |

::note
A `{requester}` label shows in the preview all the time. On stream it only appears while a song
a viewer requested is playing.
::

## Banner

Wide and short, for the top or bottom of a scene.

### Default

![Default](/img/premades/default.png){width="728" height="208" .mx-auto .rounded-xl}

Square art flush to the left edge, title and artist beside it, on an album-colored gradient.
`680 × 160`

### Minimal

![Minimal](/img/premades/minimal.png){width="462" height="88" .mx-auto .rounded-xl}

The built-in minimal theme: a slim pill with an equalizer, the title and artist either side of a
dotted divider, and a short bar. `414 × 40`

### Swag

![Swag](/img/premades/swag.png){width="728" height="213" .mx-auto .rounded-xl}

The built-in swag theme: art down the left edge, a big bold title over the artist, and a flat
bar along the bottom. `680 × 165`

### Spiffy

![Spiffy](/img/premades/spiffy.png){width="748" height="121" .mx-auto .rounded-xl}

One slim row: title, waveform, artist, and a pill-shaped bar, on an album-colored gradient. `700 × 73`

### Pocket

![Pocket](/img/premades/pocket.png){width="728" height="312" .mx-auto .rounded-xl}

A player card with the art on the right, a live dot, and a small pill of player buttons.
`680 × 264`

### Orbit Wide

![Orbit Wide](/img/premades/orbit-wide.png){width="756" height="284" .mx-auto .rounded-xl}

Orbit as a banner: the art circling at the end of a wide card, with a squiggle bar, the song
times, and the next request. `708 × 236`

### Vinyl

![Vinyl](/img/premades/vinyl.png){width="588" height="252" .mx-auto .rounded-xl}

The album art as the label of a spinning record, sticking out of its sleeve. It stops when the
music does. `540 × 204`

### Blocky

![Blocky](/img/premades/blocky.png){width="688" height="248" .mx-auto .rounded-xl}

A survival-sandbox look: the art pixelated in a stone slot, pixel text with a hard shadow, and
an XP bar for progress. `640 × 200`

### Broadcast

![Broadcast](/img/premades/broadcast.png){width="808" height="200" .mx-auto .rounded-xl}

A TV lower third with an on-air tag, and the next requests with who asked for them sliding in
underneath. `760 × 152`

### Aurora

![Aurora](/img/premades/aurora.png){width="728" height="272" .mx-auto .rounded-xl}

Your album art melted into a soft glow behind glass, with everything centered over it and a
drifting squiggle bar. `680 × 224`

### Grimoire

![Grimoire](/img/premades/grimoire.png){width="728" height="288" .mx-auto .rounded-xl}

A spell being cast: the art in a magic circle of gold rings and sparkles, on pale sky blue with
a serif title. `680 × 240`

## Panel

Roughly twice as wide as it is tall, for a corner with room to spare.

### Frostbite

![Frostbite](/img/premades/frostbite.png){width="608" height="428" .mx-auto .rounded-xl}

Frosted glass around the art and title, with a wide two-tone waveform and the times along the
bottom. `560 × 380`

### Orbit

![Orbit](/img/premades/orbit.png){width="948" height="468" .mx-auto .rounded-xl}

Round art circling above a dark card inside a thin orbit, with a squiggle bar, the song times,
and the next requests along the bottom. `900 × 420`

### Pixel Quest

![Pixel Quest](/img/premades/pixel-quest.png){width="688" height="312" .mx-auto .rounded-xl}

The album art as a blocky game world, with the song in pixel lettering over it, a row of
controls, and a green bar for progress. `640 × 264`

### Arcade

![Arcade](/img/premades/arcade.png){width="688" height="348" .mx-auto .rounded-xl}

A handheld game console: a pixelated screen, a glowing LCD title, and a D-pad with A and B
buttons. `640 × 300`

## Portrait

Taller than wide, for the side of a scene.

### Detached

![Detached](/img/premades/detached.png){width="351" height="469" .mx-auto .rounded-xl}

Art, text, and the progress bar on tiles of their own, with your scene showing between them.
`303 × 421`

### Stacked

![Stacked](/img/premades/stacked.png){width="348" height="448" .mx-auto .rounded-xl}

Art on top, centered text underneath. Good for a corner. `300 × 400`

### Snapshot

![Snapshot](/img/premades/snapshot.png){width="348" height="416" .mx-auto .rounded-xl}

An instant photo with a strip of tape and a doodled heart, captioned by hand. `300 × 368`
