The dashboard has a visual editor: **Widget themes → Open the theme editor**, or
`http://127.0.0.1:3002/editor.html`.

Drag the parts of the widget around a canvas, restyle them, and save. That writes a theme folder
under `widget/themes/`, and it shows up in `!theme` straight away. No restart, no build step.

![The theme editor](/img/theme-editor.png){width="1920" height="946" .rounded-xl}

## Starting a theme

- **Premade themes**, in the top bar, opens 18 finished designs to start from
- **New** starts a blank theme
- **Duplicate** makes a copy of the theme you have open
- The built-in themes, default, minimal, and swag, are at the bottom of the theme list under
  **Built in (read-only)**. Open one to look at it, and save it under a new name to make it your own

::

## The parts

Album art, title, artist, progress bar, time elapsed, song length, and Up next are always part
of a theme, because the widget fills them in. Hide the ones a design doesn't need rather than
deleting them.

On top of those, add as many of your own as you like:

- **Cards**, shapes to put behind or over other parts
- **Icons**, from a library of over 2,000
- **Labels**, lines of your own text

### Hiding and locking

Each part has an eye and a padlock in the parts list.

- **The eye hides a part.** A hidden part isn't on the canvas at all. Click it in the list to
  change its settings, or to show it again
- **The padlock locks a part in place.** It can't be dragged, resized, or nudged, and its
  position and size fields are frozen. Clicks go straight through it to whatever is underneath,
  which makes a card behind everything easy to work over

Both are saved with the theme.

## Selecting

- Click a part on the canvas, or its name in the parts list
- Hold :kbd{value="Ctrl"} and click to add a part to the selection, or take it out. This works
  in the parts list too
- Drag a box across the canvas from an empty spot. Everything the box touches is selected.
  Hidden and locked parts are left out
- Click an empty spot on the canvas to select the canvas itself. Click the space around it to
  select nothing

Drag any selected part to move them all together, or nudge them with the arrow keys.

## Changing several parts at once

With more than one part selected, the settings panel shows **Change all of them**. It holds the
settings every selected part has, and changing one changes it on all of them. One undo takes it
back on all of them too.

![Changing several parts at once](/img/theme-editor-several.png){width="320" height="760" .mx-auto .rounded-xl}

| You select | You can change together |
| --- | --- |
| Icons | Color, line weight, rotation, opacity, and drop shadow |
| Text parts: title, artist, times, labels, Up next | Font, size, weight, color, letter spacing, alignment, opacity, uppercase, italic, glow, outline, and drop shadow |
| Text and icons | Color, opacity, and drop shadow |
| Cards | Background, fill opacity, corners, border, shadow, frost, and bevel |
| Cards and the album art | Corners, border, and shadow |

- A setting only counts as shared when it means the same thing for each part. Text and a card
  share nothing, for example
- Where the parts are set differently, the setting says **Mixed**. Changing it sets them all to
  the same value
- On a mixed width or height, the :kbd{value="−"} and :kbd{value="+"} buttons move each part from
  its own value
- Giving several text parts one font keeps each one's weight, where the new font has it
- **Size** sets the width or height of every selected part that isn't locked

## Layers

Parts are stacked, and the ones higher up are drawn over the ones below. Every part has a
**Layer** row at the top of its settings:

- **Forward** and **Backward** move it past the next part it overlaps
- **To front** and **To back** move it all the way
- Under the buttons, it says what the part is over and under

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| :kbd{value="Ctrl"} :kbd{value="]"} | Bring forward |
| :kbd{value="Ctrl"} :kbd{value="["} | Send backward |
| :kbd{value="Ctrl"} :kbd{value="Shift"} :kbd{value="]"} | Bring to front |
| :kbd{value="Ctrl"} :kbd{value="Shift"} :kbd{value="["} | Send to back |

## The canvas

The canvas is the size the widget is in OBS. Select it by clicking an empty spot on it, or
**Canvas** in the parts list.

- Drag its right or bottom edge to resize it, or type a width and height
- Lock it with its padlock to keep its size
- The panel behind everything can be a color or a gradient, with rounded corners and a border
- Hide the panel to put the parts straight over your gameplay
- **Blur** and **Dim** soften what the panel shows, so text reads over busy album art or a Canvas
  video. They can't touch your gameplay

### Parts that stick out of the panel

Normally nothing can reach past the edge of the panel. Set **Panel inset** to draw the panel
smaller than the canvas. Anything in the space around it hangs over the panel's edge, the way the
record sticks out of its sleeve in [Vinyl](/themes/premade-themes#vinyl).

Set the top, bottom, left, and right separately. The larger the inset, the further a part can
stick out.

## Song change

How the art, title, and artist change over when the song does. Pick one under **Song change**:

- **Slide**: out to one side, in from the other
- **Fade**: a soft crossfade, with a slight zoom
- **Flip**: the art turns over like a card, and the text rolls up
- **Pop**: out small, in with a little bounce
- **Wipe**: wiped off and back on, like a TV graphic
- **None**: the song just changes

A skip back to the song before runs the other way. The colors taken from the album art blend
into the new song's rather than jumping. Try it on the preview with **Previous** and **Next**.

New themes start with Slide. Themes you made before these existed keep changing songs the way
they always did.

## Viewer rewards

Two switches for [viewer rewards](/dashboard/viewer-rewards) on this theme:

- **Reward effects**: the effect a regular's requested song starts with, or a sub's own pick.
  Under it, it says what your channel gives out
- **Milestones**: the widget steps aside for a few seconds on a viewer's milestone songs.
  **Show them for** sets how many seconds

The box under it, **Try them on this theme**, is only a preview, and nothing in it is saved. Pick
any effect and colors and press **Play effect** or **Play a milestone** to see how it looks on this
theme. On stream, each viewer gets the effect their tier or their own pick gives them.

New themes and the premades have both on. Themes you made before rewards existed don't until you
tick them. Who gets which effect is set in **Admin Panel → Viewer rewards**, not in the theme.

## Ambience

A quiet drift across the widget while music plays: **Embers**, **Snow**, or **Petals**. It's
**None** until you pick one, and then its colors, how much, and how fast show up. It plays on the
preview as you change it.

It never bursts or flashes, so viewers' rewards stay special, and it fades out while one plays.

## Album art

- Round each corner by the same amount, or each one on its own
- A border, or a gradient ring
- A shadow to lift it off the panel
- **Blur** blurs the art or Canvas video inside its own edges, which stay sharp, as does any
  border
- **Pixelate** draws it in blocks, for a game look. It works on Canvas videos too
- **Spin** turns it like a record. It stops when the music does

## Cards

Add them from the **Cards** section of the parts list. A card is a shape to put behind, or over,
any part.

- A color or a gradient, and how see-through it is
- Its corners, border, and shadow. The border can be a gradient ring
- **Bevel** makes it look like a button
- **Frost** blurs whatever of the widget is behind it, like frosted glass

## Labels

Add them from the **Labels** section. A label is a line of your own text, such as "Now playing".

Write `{requester}`, or click **Add who requested it**, and it becomes the name of the viewer who
requested the song. With no requester, the label fades out on stream.

## Icons

Over 2,000 icons from [Lucide](https://lucide.dev). Search by name, or press **Show all icons**
to scroll through every one of them. Drag one where you want it. Color, line weight, rotation,
opacity, and drop shadow are adjustable, and the color can follow the album art.

## Up next

Shows what plays next in your Spotify queue. Turn on **Up next** in the parts list.

- It only appears while something is queued, and fades in and out on its own
- Show album pictures, and who asked for each song
- Set **Show** to **Only chat requests** to leave out the rest of your playlist
- Choose how many songs, the caption, and the color and roundness of each one

## The progress bar

Three shapes:

- **Bar**, which fills as the song plays
- **Waveform**, lines of differing heights. Set how many and how far apart, and how rounded
  their ends are with **Line roundness**. **Shuffle** makes a different pattern, and it's saved
  with the theme
- **Squiggle**: the part already played is a moving wave, and the rest is a plain line

::waveform-diagram
::

The fill can be one color or a gradient, and a **Knob** can ride along the bar.

The two song times sit either side of the bar by default, `0:04` on the left and `3:07` on the
right. They're ordinary parts, so you can move, restyle, or hide them.

## Working in the editor

::snap-diagram
::

- **Snapping**: edges and centers line up as you drag, resizing locks to another part's width
  or height, and moving locks to spacing you've already used. Hold :kbd{value="Ctrl"} or
  :kbd{value="Shift"} while dragging to turn it off. The toolbar says so too
- **Search the settings**: the box at the top of the settings panel stays put as you scroll.
  Type a word like `shadow` or `radius` to see only those settings, and the parts with that
  name. Press :kbd{value="/"} to jump to it, and :kbd{value="Esc"} to clear it
- **Place in OBS** is always in the settings panel. It has buttons for `!tr` and `!bc`, so you
  can place the widget without using chat
- **Revert to last save** throws away everything since the last save. Undo still works, and
  undoes the revert itself
- **The preview plays your real song.** It's drawn by the same code that writes the theme, with
  the song playing and its Canvas video, so it matches what OBS shows. The clock and progress
  bar move every second, and a new song shows up as soon as it does in OBS

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| Arrow keys | Nudge the selection 1px |
| :kbd{value="Shift"} + arrow keys | Nudge it 10px |
| :kbd{value="Ctrl"} :kbd{value="Z"} | Undo |
| :kbd{value="Ctrl"} :kbd{value="Shift"} :kbd{value="Z"} | Redo |
| :kbd{value="Ctrl"} :kbd{value="S"} | Save |
| :kbd{value="/"} | Search the settings |

The **?** in the top bar opens a short guide to the editor.

::note
Themes you make live in `widget/themes/` and are left alone by updates.
::
